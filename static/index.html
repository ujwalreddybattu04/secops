CALCULATION METHODS

Choose what your yearly number means.

Average · a yearly mean

A 6% yearly input means the average of that year's 12 monthly values is 6% before rounding. The optimizer chooses a smooth profile across the full timeline, with a soft penalty for moving outside that column's yearly range.

Its first month is constrained to start positive. Other months can be negative or exceed the yearly range. Tiny positive values may display as 0% after rounding.

Exit · a year-end target

A 6% yearly input means December is 6%. The optimizer solves the other months together to minimize changes in the curve's slope. Only the first month is constrained to be non-negative; later values can leave the yearly range.

One non-negative year gives a flat curve. With multiple years, the first year's shape also depends on the later targets.

These are estimated monthly profiles, not forecasts learned from observed monthly data. The graph connects the generated values without adding extra smoothing.
